# We're hiring a part-time design intern in London {#design-intern}

_Hard Problems is hiring a paid intern for 9 months to help us with design, writing, and producing a podcast. You'll get lots of hands-on experience and mentorship._{.intro}

![Illustration inviting you to join Hard Problems](/images/content/illustration-join-us.svg){.float-right}

## About Hard Problems {#about-hard-problems}

Good design solves problems. Great design solves problems that matter.

Hard Problems is a UK non-profit that helps designers, researchers, and product people find work on the things that count — public health, climate change, poverty, education, good government. We run a curated job board, publish articles and interviews, and host a coworking space in Shoreditch for teams working on climate and global health.

We're small, which means you'll do real work from week one and see it ship.

## The design intern role {#the-design-intern-role}

You'll be our design intern for nine months, working across everything we make: the website, the job board, our content, and a new podcast we're launching. This is a broad role. If you like being handed a problem and figuring it out (with regular feedback from two senior designers), you'll enjoy it.

### What you'll do

- **Curate the job board.** Find and add jobs at organisations working on hard problems. You'll use and improve some AI tools as well as using ingenuity and your judgement — knowing which jobs are genuinely worth surfacing.
- **Write and edit.** Draft content for the Hard Problems website: articles, interviews, newsletters.
- **Design new features** for the website, starting with our new podcast section. You'll get to use design tools as well as designing in code, if you can.
- **Help produce the podcast.** We're launching a show interviewing designers around the world who work on high-impact problems like healthcare and climate change. You'll help make it happen.
- **User research and prototyping.** We are considering a new service offering and need your help to shape the strategy and offering.
- **Graphic design** for the podcast, the site, and whatever else we're building.
- **Learn AI coding tools.** Not required, but we use them and we'd like you to.

### What we're looking for

- A strong writer. This matters more than anything else on this list.
- Good UI and UX skills. We're tool-agnostic, though Figma experience is handy.
- Front-end coding is a bonus — you can learn on the job.
- Passion for improving design as a profession.

You don't need a design degree or previous internships. Show us things you've made. If you don't tick every box, apply anyway. We'd rather see your work than your checklist.

## Who you'll work with {#who-youll-work-with}

You'll work directly with Hard Problems' small founding team: [Elyce Cole](/authors/elyce-cole) (org psychologist), [Mahima Chandak](/authors/mahima-chandak) (designer), and [Daniel Burka](/authors/daniel-burka) (designer). There's no layer of management between you and the people making decisions. It is currently just the three of us and you.

You'll report directly to one of the co-founders, [Daniel Burka](/authors/daniel-burka), who has a long history in design at organisations like Google Ventures, Mozilla, and Resolve to Save Lives. Expect regular one-to-ones and frequent feedback on your work.

## Details {#details}

- **Term:** 9 months, part-time (4 hours per day, 5 days per week)
- **Start date:** 1 October 2026
- **Location:** London. Hybrid, with at least some days each week in our Shoreditch office (1 Rivington Place).
- **Compensation:**
  - **Pay:** £12,750 for the 9-month internship (This is equivalent to £34,000 full-time salary for a year, but for half time work over 9 months)
  - **Holiday:** 14 days plus bank holidays
  - **Pension:** Workplace pension with employer contributions
- **Equipment:** We'll provide a Mac laptop and a large monitor if you don't already have them.
- **Right to work:** You must have the right to work in the UK. We cannot sponsor visas for this role.
