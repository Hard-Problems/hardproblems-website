# Fellowships to help you shift into a career designing for impact {#fellowships-to-help-you-shift-into-a-career-designing-for-impact}

_It took me a decade of wrong turns to land the career that I actually wanted. These fellowships can get you there in months._{.intro}

Back in 2016 when I graduated as a designer, there was no real path for me to apply my skills to problems that I cared about. The shiniest jobs were in advertising, but that didn't appeal to me. Instead, I joined a non-profit that works with women in shelter homes as… a project coordinator. The work mattered. But I wanted a formal design role in an organisation that was doing good in the world. And I wondered, "How hard could that be to find?"

It was hard.

It took me another gig in early childhood education, followed by a master's degree in designing for impact, plus a couple of internships, to finally land my first big break as a designer on a large-scale public health project.

A decade later, I see fellowships offering what it took me years to figure out on my own. They build bridges for designers who want to work on hard problems, and are a low-risk way to test the waters. What skills can you carry over? What additional domain knowledge do you require? Who else has walked this path before you?

Whether you're just starting off in design or weighing a career switch, here's our handpicked list of fellowships for good to consider.

## Fellowships for <span class="highlight-underline">early career designers</span> {#early-career-designers}

### [Bharat Digital](https://www.bharatdigital.io) {#bharat-digital}

**Paid • 6 months**

Run as a non-profit, Bharat Digital embeds early career technologists inside Indian government departments. Imagine getting to improve public health programs or enhance roadways right out of college. Pretty cool, I think! I also know the founder, Harsh Nisar, goes out of his way to get fellows hired once the fellowship is over. This is exactly the kind of program I could have benefitted from early on in my career.

[![The Bharat Digital website — "Design for Bharat"](/images/content/fellowship-bharat.jpg){.normal-width}](https://www.bharatdigital.io)

### [US Digital Corps](https://digitalcorps.gsa.gov/opportunity/) {#us-digital-corps}

**Paid • 2 years**

There are certain skills that will help you advance in your career, especially in the public sector. Skills like communicating technical concepts to a general audience, connecting the dots between various disciplines as the generalist in the room, and influencing a group to be user-centric. I personally love that this fellowship is all about learning these real-world skills in an immersive 2-year program.

[![The US Digital Corps website — "Begin your career at the intersection of technology and public service"](/images/content/fellowship-us-digital-corps.jpg){.normal-width}](https://digitalcorps.gsa.gov/opportunity/)

### [Coding it Forward](https://codingitforward.com/fellowship) {#coding-it-forward}

**Paid • 10 months**

If you can't commit two years yet, Coding it Forward is an equally good option to dabble around for a few months. What caught my attention is that 55% of their alumni continue to work full-time in social impact. Surely they're doing this right! [Their GitHub](https://github.com/codingitforward) has a full archive of past projects.

[![The Coding it Forward website — "Investing in the next generation. Building tech that serves the people."](/images/content/fellowship-coding-it-forward.jpg){.normal-width}](https://codingitforward.com/fellowship)

## Fellowships for <span class="highlight-underline">mid-career designers</span> {#mid-career-designers}

### [UX for Change](https://www.ux4change.org/responsible-design-for-change-fellowship/) {#ux-for-change}

**Tuition based (£875 + VAT) • 4 months**

If you're at that stage of your career where you're feeling a lack of purpose at work and questioning your next move, UX for Change might be the right place for you. For the first 5 weeks, a coach guides you to unlearn, reflect, and build your understanding of responsible design. For the rest of the fellowship, you apply your skills and build meaningful solutions for [East London Water Works](https://elwp.org.uk/Home.htm), as an individual contributor or leading the team of ICs.

[![The UX for Change website — "Responsible Design for Change Fellowship"](/images/content/fellowship-ux-for-change.jpg){.normal-width}](https://www.ux4change.org/responsible-design-for-change-fellowship/)

### [Climatebase](https://www.climatebase.org/fellowship) {#climatebase}

**Tuition based ($1990) • Financial aid available • 3 months**

Like most hard problems, the climate crisis cannot be solved just by technologists alone. It requires us to collaborate with scientists, policy experts, investors, founders, activists and more. The Climatebase fellowship is designed for you to learn from these experts as well as grow your professional network beyond tech. In my opinion, speaking the language of a diverse group will give your climate career a solid edge.

[![The Climatebase Fellowship website — "Accelerate your climate career"](/images/content/fellowship-climatebase.jpg){.normal-width}](https://www.climatebase.org/fellowship)

## Fellowships for <span class="highlight-underline">leaders & founders</span> {#leaders-and-founders}

### [School of Moral Ambition](https://www.moralambition.org/fellowships/all-fellowships) {#school-of-moral-ambition}

**Paid • 7 months**

I discovered this fellowship through the Moral Ambition book (we even wrote a [book review](/articles/moral-ambition-rutger-bregman)). The book's main proposition is that there are countless ways to improve the world. But if you want to make a meaningful impact, choose a [problem that is sizable, solvable, and sorely overlooked](https://www.moralambition.org/stories/sizable-solvable-sorely-overlooked). This thinking is reflected in the fellowship which focuses on problems like tax fairness, tobacco-free future, and food system reform. Their team has also put out a helpful [webinar](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1MEBa0USPNgSehXk4Rx7BPrpVkgPk5VuV/view) covering their selection process and what the fellowship looks like. Bookmark it for later!

[![The School for Moral Ambition website — "Moral Ambition Fellowships"](/images/content/fellowship-moral-ambition.jpg){.normal-width}](https://www.moralambition.org/fellowships/all-fellowships)

### [Harvard Loeb Fellowship](https://www.gsd.harvard.edu/loeb-fellowship/) {#harvard-loeb-fellowship}

**Paid • 1 year**

The Loeb Fellowship is the least structured program on this list, but in the best possible way. A group of 10 design leaders (design used in the broadest sense to include policymakers, social entrepreneurs, architects, civic leaders, and so on) develop their individual work plan based on their goals. In this intense 1-year program, you can attend any course at Harvard or MIT, use their library systems, and participate in lectures, conferences, exhibitions. A close knit group imagining how to make the world better for all sounds like a perfect way to step away from your professional life for one academic year.

[![The Harvard Graduate School of Design website — "Loeb Fellowship"](/images/content/fellowship-loeb.jpg){.normal-width}](https://www.gsd.harvard.edu/loeb-fellowship/)
